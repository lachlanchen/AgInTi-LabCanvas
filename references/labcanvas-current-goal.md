  open that card and copy its native share link, then download the original and
  transcribe on the configured GPU 1 route. Return the video, transcription, and
  concise summary. Do not ask for a link before trying the native route.
- Card-only input does not guarantee success when the client cannot open the
  card or the source is unavailable. Report the actual failure stage; never
  substitute another video, a recording, or a title-derived summary presented
  as a transcript.
- Gongzhonghao cards use exact full-text recovery and source-grounded analysis.
- An ordinary video without an instruction is save-only, not publication.
  Publishing requires current same-chat authorization, correct video identity,
  and context correction of subtitles and metadata through LazyEdit.
- A direct long-running request should get one useful brief acknowledgement,
  then meaningful progress or a blocker when appropriate, and a verified final
  result. Never spam logs or repeated receipts.
