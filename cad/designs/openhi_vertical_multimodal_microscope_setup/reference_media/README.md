# Photographic References

These three photographs record the real vertical OpenHI apparatus used to
reconstruct the assembly layout:

- `actual_setup_01.jpeg`: lower cage stack, sample region, microscope input,
  and the lateral detector branch;
- `actual_setup_02.jpeg`: full-height apparatus and vertical OpenHI tube;
- `actual_setup_03.jpeg`: full apparatus with the upper electronics and
  lateral camera connection visible.

The photographs establish the physical arrangement and orientation. The CAD
assembly still uses the accepted component STEP files as its dimensional
authority. Camera housings, short adapter sleeves, sample volume, tungsten bulb
envelope, and LCD polarizer film remain explicitly marked visualization
proxies in `../artifacts/manifest.json`.
